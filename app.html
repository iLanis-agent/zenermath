Zener Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out series resistors and dissipations for a shunt zener regulator from the numbers you type: the drop over the current for the resistor, the drop squared over the resistor for its heat, and the zener voltage times the full current for the no-load case. The voltages, currents and resistors are yours to measure or read off your own circuit and datasheet. It treats the zener voltage as fixed, so it has not seen your circuit and does not know the knee, the tolerance, the temperature drift or the part variant, and it gives no verdict that a regulator circuit is adequate or safe. Every number here is your own entry.

The resistor truth

The series resistor that sets the current: the drop over the target current.

The power truth

What the resistor burns: the current through it and its dissipation.

The no-load truth

The case that cooks zeners: the load disconnects and the zener takes the full current.
